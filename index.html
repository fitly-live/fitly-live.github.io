About Fitly

Fitly is a social fitness platform designed to help people stay consistent by connecting them with compatible workout partners, personalized fitness programs, nutrition guidance, and local sports communities. Whether you are looking for someone to train with, organize a basketball game, or follow a structured workout plan, Fitly brings fitness, accountability, and community together in one modern mobile app.
